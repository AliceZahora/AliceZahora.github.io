History of the Art

Since before humans began communicating with each other through recognizable language, painting has existed as a form of expression. From cave art to digital works, the craft has evolved throughout the years it has existed. As depicted below, numerous major evolutions were seperated into eras, each having a unique aspect that sets them apart. With the modern age came the introduction of technology in art. While digital paintings may not be considered the same as physical one, the programs they are created with have become increasingly accurate with recreating the appearance of canvas and brush.

Personal Experience

My family has always been a fan of the arts, whether that be drawing, dancing, playing instruments, or singing. While our talents don't necessarily always reflect our passion, we enjoy the hobbies nonetheless. Because of this, I was always encouraged to explore drawing in my free time, which inevitably led to my introduction to painting as well. However, I never made a big effort in that medium until around college, when I began making portrait paintings as gifts.

Because I've never really been trained, I'm sure my methods are less than ideal. A true artist would probably cringe at my technique, but I'm happy if it at gets the job done. Suprisingly, I have very little patience for tutorials, so unfortunately, any improvements I make are slow going and only due to practice. As soon as I think about being taught, it transforms the hobby into a bit of a chore. I'd rather be not as skilled but enjoy what I'm doing rather than more talented but hating it. I'd love if I could have the best of both worlds, but sadly, I'm mentally not quite at that point.
