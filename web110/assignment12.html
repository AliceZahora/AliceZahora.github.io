Summary Paragraph

  Due to its integral position as one of the three core aspects of web coding, knowing how to utilize JavaScript is a much-needed skill. This lesson introduced numerous usages for said language. Inserting information into HTML elements, dynamically retrieving and displaying the date, and drawing graphics on canvases -- each showcased through the activities and each an important ability to have. For example, the information displayed on pages is often not static, needing to be updated or depending on outside factors to be set. This is made possible with JavaScript, as taught in the activities. The language is intertwined strongly with website building, and for that reason, its presence will more often than not grace any future projects. Therefore, the lessons will serve as useful tools not just now, but for a long time going forward.
