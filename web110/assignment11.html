  Multimedia is an integral part of many websites, their videos, images, and audio creating unique user experiences. Whether they are for aesthetic reasons or work in tandem with the site’s purpose, their presence makes a big difference. It is because of this that learning how to implement these features is important. Through the lessons this week, I gained knowledge on the differences between the various types of media, as well as how each is integrated. Knowing how to accommodate different browsers, manipulate appearances/usages, identify the advantages of alternate image formats, etc. All vital skills that will no doubt be utilized in almost every project going forward.
