Web110 Assignment 9

  For various skills in life, there is a difference between doing something and doing something well. When it comes to web development, it is important to not just create websites, but to ensure that they follow the proper standards and common practices. It is for this reason that the activities for this week were very helpful. Building a solid foundation can be done through reviewing the first activity’s introduction to HTML. Whether someone is new to the subject or well versed, is it imperative to build projects on the backs of a strong understanding of the basics. If the foundations are sloppy, the rest falls. Similarly, it is important to know how to debug, as covered in the third activity. It is inevitable that, sometimes, things don’t work out as we planned them. In knowing how to debug, errors can not only be identified and fixed, but avoided too. Having a resource for validation as provided in the lesson is a helpful tool as well. However, it is the hosting of websites that truly makes these skills a necessity. For that reason, activity four’s coverage of different alternatives is important to keep in mind. In the future, when there is a need for hosting a site, that knowledge will come in handy.
