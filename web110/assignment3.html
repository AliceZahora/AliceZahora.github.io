  Among the various activities I completed for this week’s lesson, the ones that I anticipate having the greatest impact on either my present or future were “Learn about Search Engine Optimization (SEO),” “Learn about Artificial Intelligence (AI),” and “Use the Internet Archive” (numbers 4, 5, and 6, respectively). Though I had a shallow understanding of how search engines ranked pages in order to display results for their users, the more in-depth coverage provided by the lesson gave me insights I hope to utilize in the future. For example, I had not previously known that backlinks impact a page’s ranking, nor was I aware of how search engines work to compensate for manipulative tactics to artificially raise it. As someone hoping to enter the field of web development, knowing how best to reach intended userbases is an integral ability to have.

  With the surge of AI in recent years, the need to understand it has become more important than ever. This doesn’t just mean knowing how to use it, but also becoming familiar with its impact and inner workings. Through the activity, I was able to learn not only about the security, consent, and accuracy risks of such technology, but I was also introduced to the strategies to mitigate those issues. The more that AI is relied on, the more important it is to understand that it, just like other information sources on the internet, is not infallible and must be used with care. For example, in exploring ChatGPT for the activity, I attempted to have it identify the episode source of a scene from a show. It very confidently told me not just an incorrect episode, but it combined summaries and gave overall bad information. My inquiry was unimportant in the grand scheme of things, but it's an indication of a larger problem. It isn’t just that the information can be wrong, but the confidence with which the answer is given may very easily fool someone into blindly trusting it. However, when used properly/responsibly and taken with a grain of salt, AI is a powerful tool and one that will likely become a staple of technology. Therefore, I am grateful for this activity for teaching me more about it.

  The Internet Archive is no doubt a very helpful tool. Therefore, knowing how to best utilize it is a useful skill to have. While I have taken advantage of it in the past, I have never uploaded my own page to the site. Through the activity, I was able to practice that and take a deeper dive into the Archive. When it comes to history, having tangible reminders of what used to be is valued -- fossils, pictures, videos. That is no different when it comes to the internet. It is fascinating to be able to practically time-travel and see a webpage as it was decades ago. Knowing not only how to access that knowledge but also contribute to it is information I am grateful to have gained.
