    When it comes to websites, there is importance not just in the contents of a page, but its presentation. The organization/layout, style, and overall appearance can have a great effect on a website's ease of use and user impressions (among other things). Bad looking websites can often deter users, giving the impression of untrustworthiness or unprofessionalism. But more importantly, a lack of structure can make them difficult to use. Without attention to layout, finding what is needed becomes an arduous task. That is why this week’s activities were vital. Knowing how to utilize CSS is the key to solving the aforementioned issues and making sure a site is organized, easy to navigate, and appealing -- a skill that will impact each webpage I create, both now and in the future.
